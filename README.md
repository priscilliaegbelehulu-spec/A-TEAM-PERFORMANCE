# A-TEAM-PERFORMANCE
An interactive performance dashboard to monitor First-Time Attendees (FTAs), track follow-up progress, evaluate team workload, and measure member engagement.
## Project Overview

An automated data analytics and business intelligence solution developed to track first-time attendees (FTAs), manage follow-up workflows, monitor A-Team performance, and analyze membership interest. Built with Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, and Looker Studio.

## Project Objective

To design and implement an automated attendee management and performance-monitoring system that streamlines FTA assignments, facilitates follow-up communication, tracks engagement outcomes, and provides actionable insights into team workload and membership interest. The project demonstrates how backend automation and interactive dashboards can improve operational efficiency and support data-driven decision-making.
## Tools & Technologies

i. Google Sheets

ii. Google Apps Script

iii. Looker Studio

iv. Calculated fields and KPI development

## Backend Implementation

i. FTA ID Generation: Automatically generates unique, date-based attendee IDs.

ii. Team Member Management: Generates A-Team member IDs and synchronizes member records with form options.

iii. Automated Assignment: Assigns FTAs to active team members based on current workload and maximum capacity.

iv. Assignment Logging: Records assignment details, timestamps, and assignment status.

v. Email Automation: Sends welcome emails to FTAs and assignment notifications to team members.

vi.  Feedback and Load Management: Processes follow-up feedback and supports workload tracking.

vii. Master Table Generation: Integrates attendee, assignment, team member, and feedback data into a consolidated reporting table.

viii. Refresh Metadata: Automatically updates the dashboard's last-refreshed timestamp.

## Key Findings

. 21 FTAs recorded, with 14 M&G attendances and a 67% follow-up completion rate.

. 61.9% of FTAs were classified as “Not Yet” regarding membership interest.

. Friends were the leading referral source, accounting for 7 FTAs.

Team assignments ranged from 1 to 6 FTAs per member.

Experience ratings: overall 4.3, worship 4.4, and Word 4.6.

Average response time was 65.14 hours.

Recommendations

Improve follow-up completion and response speed.

Develop targeted engagement strategies for undecided attendees.

Balance assignments based on workload and capacity.

Strengthen data quality and standardize KPI calculations.
